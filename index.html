Bengaluru tanker guide

Useful categories for a local marketplace: Cauvery/treated municipal supply, borewell water, and construction/non-potable water. Common search sizes include 4,000–6,000 L small loads and 8,000–12,000 L apartment loads; larger 16,000 L+ tankers should be confirmed with the operator. Rates vary by source, locality, distance, pumping and season.
